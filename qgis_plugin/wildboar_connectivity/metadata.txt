about=Interactive plugin for the BA "Wild Boar Connectivity Modeling" thesis.
    Select a resistance raster and the "Kerneinstaende" habitats polygon
    layer, click one habitat on the map, set a wild boar range (default
    5 km), and the plugin computes pairwise least-cost paths and a
    cumulative Circuitscape-style current-flow / pinchpoint raster across
    every pair of habitats inside the buffer. Restricting the analysis
    to one local neighbourhood is ecologically defensible since wild boar
    home ranges and dispersal distances rarely span an entire canton.
