about=Decision-support tool for hunting administrations preparing for
    African Swine Fever (ASF). Pick a resistance raster and the
    "Kerneinstaende" habitat polygons, click one habitat as the
    outbreak origin, and the plugin computes (a) the cumulative
    least-cost paths a wild boar would take to neighbouring habitats,
    (b) a Circuitscape current-flow / pinchpoint raster showing where
    boar movement concentrates, (c) a per-habitat risk score showing
    which habitats are vulnerable. The user can then DRAW FENCES or
    PLACE WILDLIFE OVERPASSES interactively on the canvas - the
    plugin applies those modifications to a working copy of the
    resistance surface and re-runs the analysis. The goal is to find
    where a fence segment would be most effective at slowing ASF.
